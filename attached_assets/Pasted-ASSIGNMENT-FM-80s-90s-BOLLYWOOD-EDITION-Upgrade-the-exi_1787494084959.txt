🔗 YOUTUBE PLAYBACK — VERY IMPORTANT

DO NOT download songs.

DO NOT convert YouTube videos to MP3.

DO NOT use:

new Audio("https://youtube.com/...")

YouTube URLs are not direct audio files.

Implement playback using the YouTube embedded player / IFrame Player API.

The YouTube player should be controlled by the Assignment FM UI.

When user clicks:

▶ Play

the selected YouTube video should play.

The music player should support:

- Play
- Pause
- Next
- Previous
- Volume
- Progress
- Current song
- Queue
- Playlist
- Song switching

The player should NOT be muted by default.

Playback must be triggered by the user's click.

---

🚨 EMBED FALLBACK

Some YouTube videos may not allow embedding.

Detect playback/embed errors.

If a song cannot be embedded, show:

Button:

Open on YouTube ↗

Do not break the entire player.
