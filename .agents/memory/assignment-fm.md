---
name: Assignment FM direction
description: The durable product and visual direction for the Assignment FM music app.
---

Assignment FM is intentionally a frontend-first campus radio experience for engineering students, not an admin dashboard or an imitation of a mainstream streaming product.

**Why:** The product's shareability depends on personality and emotional recognition: it should feel like the soundtrack to a real student's late-night work session.

**How to apply:** Preserve the editorial, warm, slightly irreverent voice when adding playlists, songs, and future screens. Continue using official third-party music links rather than hosting copyrighted audio.
